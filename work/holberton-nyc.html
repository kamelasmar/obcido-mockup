Holberton NYC case study: website, PPC & social for a coding school

Filling coding cohorts with a sharper site and smarter ads.

Holberton School trains software engineers. Its New York campus needed more of the right applicants finding it, understanding it and signing up.

The challenge

Holberton wanted to strengthen its online presence and reach more aspiring software engineers. That meant a website that made courses and admissions easy to understand, search visibility it didn't yet have, and campaigns that found the right people instead of everyone.

What we did

  1. Rebuilt the website around the applicant

    A more user-friendly site with direct paths to course details, admissions and programme information, and SEO built in from the start.

  2. Ran targeted PPC campaigns

    We identified the demographics most likely to enrol and placed ads where they were already searching for a way into tech.

  3. Turned students into the story on social

    Achievements, student success and course launches, published to build a community around the school, not just an audience.

Extremely creative, professional, and results-driven. They worked with us thoroughly.
