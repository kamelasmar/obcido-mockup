Growth FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand named and cited in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. Buyers now use both, so we run them as one programme.

How long until we see results?

Paid social and PPC can move numbers within weeks. SEO and GEO typically compound over three to six months.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No honest agency can guarantee rankings or AI citations. We commit to the work, the targets and monthly reporting on every number that matters.

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your market and goals. We recommend a budget after the audit, and start small enough to learn before scaling what works.
